## Calendar sprites

Files under `app/src/main/assets/` come from
[Mive82/Persona-5-Calendar](https://github.com/Mive82/Persona-5-Calendar).
The upstream repository credits **BHDown** for high-resolution calendar artwork,
**Dartemil** for the earlier Rainmeter skin, and the original Persona games.

The original artwork announcement is
[High Resolution Calendar Assets and Rainmeter Skins](https://www.reddit.com/r/Persona5/comments/bjldpg/high_resolution_calendar_assets_and_rainmeter/).

The upstream repository includes a WTFPL notice. A copy is preserved in
[`docs/licenses/Persona-5-Calendar.txt`](docs/licenses/Persona-5-Calendar.txt).
That notice is not a claim that the original game's artwork or trademarks
belong to this project or are licensed by ATLUS/SEGA under MIT or WTFPL.

The app composites the supplied sprite layers and adjusts colors and alpha
for the day-transition appearance. Weekday text remains white; weekend
numbers are blue for Saturday and red for Sunday.

## Dagger and preview images

`app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/dagger_original.png` is a transparent
Persona-style dagger supplied by the project owner. Its original external
download location was not provided. It is third-party artwork, not original

`widget_preview.png` and `docs/images/widget-preview.png` are generated from
the same calendar and dagger artwork. They are subject to the same artwork
